## C01 — United States v. Dellinger, 472 F.2d 340

- 发布者：U.S. Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit；Justia 转载。
- 裁判日期：1972-11-21；1973-03-05 为最高法院拒绝调卷日期，转载页顶部的日期不能直接作为上诉裁判日期。
- 来源：<https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/472/340/368222/>
- 定位：V. COMMUNICATIONS BY THE TRIAL JUDGE AND MARSHAL WITH THE DELIBERATING JURY.

> The deliberations began before noon on Saturday, February 14, 1970, and ended Wednesday morning, February 18, when a verdict was returned finding all seven who were then defendants not guilty of conspiracy, and finding the five present defendants guilty of the respective substantive counts.

> There was also testimony to support defendants' allegation that a marshal volunteered comments to the jury during its deliberations. Five jurors testified that they remembered the marshal saying something to the effect of, "The judge can keep you here as long as he wants."

> Under the circumstances related, we are unable to find with certainty that these out of court communications were harmless, and therefore conclude they are grounds for reversal.
